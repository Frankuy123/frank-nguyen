Prologue

You wake up on a Saturday morning and head to the kitchen to feed Mango, your dog. However, when you call his name, there is no response.

Mango's bowl is untouched, and the back door is wide open. Mango has gone missing! You need to gather clues, check nearby areas, and ask people for help before time runs out.
